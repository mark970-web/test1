Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information VacayYay collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using VacayYay, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile & listing content: any profile photo, property photos, descriptions, prices, and the contact email you choose to share on a listing.
  • Activity: bookings you make, properties you save to your wishlist, and reviews you write.
  • Local storage: we store your login token and saved-spots list in your browser to keep you signed in and remember your wishlist.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features: authentication, listings, bookings, wishlists, and reviews.
  • To display your name and photo alongside your listings and reviews.
  • To let guests contact a host via the host's chosen contact email.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

3. Passwords & Security

Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed with bcrypt before being saved. Authentication uses signed tokens stored in your browser. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is perfectly secure.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Certain information is shared as a normal part of using the platform — for example, your host contact email is shared with a signed-in guest who chooses to contact you, and your name and photo appear publicly on your listings and reviews.

5. Your Choices

  • You can change your email or password at any time from Account Settings.
  • You can remove saved spots and delete your own listings whenever you like.
  • You can permanently delete your account, which removes your listings, bookings, saved spots, and reviews.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

VacayYay uses your browser's local storage (not advertising cookies) to remember your session and wishlist. Clearing your browser storage will sign you out and remove your locally cached saved spots.

7. Children

VacayYay is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@vacayyay.example.
